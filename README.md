A collection of independently developed financial models covering banking, aviation and other industries, with a focus on integrated financial forecasting, operating performance, credit risk and capital allocation.

About

I have a BSc and MSc in Economics from the University of Sheffield and professional experience in credit risk modelling, financial analysis and quantitative modelling within the UK banking sector.

This repository documents my independent financial modelling projects, developed to extend my experience in credit risk and quantitative analysis into broader financial statement forecasting, corporate finance and investment analysis.
